## why this is good homework
Two independent derivations converged: the Utah Rivers Council's 4,200 Project arrived at
its number from lake health; this package targets 4,205, arrived at from litigation
exposure. Five feet apart, by two different roads, neither knowing about the other.
Friends of Great Salt Lake has the package. The drafting hook is extension of live
statute, not invention — the state's own Agricultural Water Optimization Act, and Title
54's brand-new large-load chapter.

## the three bills, in pitch order

**1 · DUSTY SPURS — Agricultural Water Transition Amendments.** Amends 73-10g (verified
live). Pays the capital cost of converting an irrigated acre, lends the rest at 3%, leases
the freed water instream to the lake. Names no crop. Rides on **$152M already appropriated
and unobligated.** Farm first, lake second, stated on the label. The only bill of the
three with a constituency that gains — lead with it.

**2 · SPURS v2 — Basin Consumptive Use Amendments.** Enacts 73-3-33 (verified vacant).
Charges *metered* consumptive use — water withdrawn and not returned — at the published
commercial rate, evenhandedly, wherever the product goes. v1 charged by destination and
died on *Sporhase*; v2 charges non-return and never asks the destination. **No farmer
pays:** estimated (coefficient-based) use is expressly out; agriculture participates
through Dusty Spurs, where it is paid rather than charged. Metered-vs-estimated is an
administrability line, not a geographic one.

**3 · BOOTS — Thermal Load Facility Requirements.** Enacts a new Title 54 chapter
(⚠ renumber: Ch. 25 is occupied; first vacant is 54-28 — see BOOTS-AMENDMENTS-v2.md
Change 0). No basin water for heat rejection; bring 150% of your own load in renewables;
36 hours of storage (technology-neutral, safe-harbor list attached); grid support for
compensation; and the below-grade amendment — racks under the ground, the surface handed
to the town for whatever it chooses. The facility that complies gives the town a park.

## the one sentence
We already set the money aside, here is how it pays for itself, and nobody who can't
measure their water gets charged for it.
